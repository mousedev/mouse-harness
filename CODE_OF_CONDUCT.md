# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1. The short form:

- Be direct about code and careful about people. Review the change, not the author.
- No harassment, no slurs, no publishing anyone's private information, no sexualised language or imagery, and no sustained disruption of a thread after a maintainer has asked for it to stop.
- Disagreement is expected. The benchmark numbers in particular invite scrutiny, and pointed questions about method are welcome; contempt is not.
- Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. They will say why.

This applies in the repository, its discussions, and anywhere someone is representing the project.

Report a problem to pete@mouse.dev. Reports are read by the maintainer, kept private, and answered.
