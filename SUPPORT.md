# Support

Mouse is maintained by one person alongside the hosted product, so the fastest path is a well-formed issue.

- **Something broke:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/mousedev/mouse-harness/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Include the `mouse doctor` output and the trace records it asks for; nine times out of ten the answer is in them.
- **How do I:** [Discussions](https://github.com/mousedev/mouse-harness/discussions). The docs under [`docs/`](docs/index.md) cover the loop, configuration, permissions, and the trace format; check there first.
- **Benchmark questions:** [docs/evals.md](docs/evals.md) has the numbers and the rules for quoting them; [evals/README.md](evals/README.md) is the runbook. Every published run ships its evidence under [`evals/runs/`](evals/runs/).
- **Security:** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not open a public issue.
- **OpenCode itself:** Mouse drives the `opencode` binary; a problem inside OpenCode belongs upstream at [anomalyco/opencode](https://github.com/anomalyco/opencode).

There is no paid support for the harness. The hosted product at [mouse.dev](https://www.mouse.dev) is separate.
